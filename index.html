Senior Scientist · Naver Labs Europe

CV ↓ LinkedIn ↗

I am a Senior Scientist and Project Lead at Naver Labs Europe, where I work on Embodied AI with a focus on end-to-end learning and vision-based approaches for robot navigation. My work spans reinforcement learning, computer vision, and autonomous systems. Previously I was Laboratory Manager at Xerox Research Center Europe and Senior Scientist at Philips Research. I hold a PhD from EPFL and was a PostDoc at the Redwood Center for Theoretical Neuroscience, UC Berkeley.
